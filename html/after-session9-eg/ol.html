Steps to book a movie ticket on BookMyShow

  1. Open the BookMyShow website or app.
  2. Select your city or location.
  3. Search for the movie you want to watch.
  4. Select the movie from the search results.
  5. Choose your preferred cinema or theatre.
  6. Select the date and show time.
  7. Select the number of tickets you want to book.
  8. Choose your preferred seats.
  9. Review the movie, theatre, show time, seats, and total amount.
  10. Click on the option to proceed to payment.
  11. Choose a payment method such as UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking.
  12. Complete the payment.
  13. Receive the booking confirmation and ticket details.
